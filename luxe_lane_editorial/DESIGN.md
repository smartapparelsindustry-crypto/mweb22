## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the understated majesty of high-couture South Asian and contemporary Western pret-a-porter. The aesthetic marries classical editorial print layouts—reminiscent of bespoke atelier lookbooks—with the frictionless utility of modern luxury e-commerce.

The visual direction centers on **Warm Minimalist Editorial**:
- Generous breathing room and architectural whitespace that frame garments like museum artifacts.
- Tactile, warm ivory surfaces rather than stark, clinical tech-whites, mimicking natural raw silk, unbleached cotton, and fine linen paper.
- Crisp, deliberate contrast between statuesque, high-contrast serif headlines and hyper-functional, modern geometric sans-serif metadata.
- An aura of exclusivity, poise, and artisanal craftsmanship that appeals to discerning domestic and diaspora patrons seeking bespoke Eastern silhouettes (formal ghararas, hand-embroidered raw silk kurtas) alongside sharp Western tailoring.

## Colors

The palette relies on tactile, warm organic tones accented with antique metallics and muted blush tones to evoke hand-loomed textiles, zardozi embroidery, and quiet luxury.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - `Base Canvas`: Warm Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) serves as the primary atmospheric backdrop.
  - `Pure Canvas`: Crisp Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) reserved for active form controls, modal dialogs, and high-key product showcase cards.
  - `Tonal Layering`: Warm Alabaster (`#F5EFEB`) and Atelier Card Fill (`#FAF7F2`) for structural zoning, section alternation, and subtle inset backgrounds.
- **Ink & Typography:**
  - `Primary Ink`: Obsidian Noir (`#181716`) provides unyielding contrast for editorial headers, primary action buttons, and dominant brand marks.
  - `Secondary Ink`: Charcoal Slate (`#3D3A37`) delivers readable, softer body copy, editorial summaries, and secondary product attributes.
  - `Muted Ink`: Sand Ash (`#78736E`) governs auxiliary UI labels, breadcrumbs, and SKU identifiers.
- **Accents & Precious Metallics:**
  - `Primary Accent`: Antique Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) symbolizes couture heritage, used sparingly for active filters, star ratings, premium seals, and artisanal badges.
  - `Secondary Accent`: Soft Muted Rose / Blush (`#DFC2B8` / `#E8D5CE`) adds warmth to hover states, secondary tags, and seasonal campaign highlights.
- **Surfaces & Dividers:**
  - `Hairline Outlines`: Silk Sand (`#EFE9E1`) forms razor-thin, delicate borders (0.5px to 1px) to partition editorial spaces without visual weight.
  - `Warm Sand`: (`#DFD7CD`) used for subtle divider rules and inactive form borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary grace of **Playfair Display** with the neutral, high-legibility geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Rules
- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for lookbook slogans, collection titles, product display names, and section overtures. Headings in all-caps must always employ widened tracking (`0.10em` to `0.18em`) to preserve haute couture delicacy.
- **Operational UI & Commerce Layer (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers product grids, filter sidebars, cart workflows, sizing tables, and checkout fields.
- **Currency & Pricing Treatment:** Pakistani Rupee prices always lead with the currency code in medium weight followed by formatted numerals (e.g., `PKR 24,500`). Slashes and decimal points are omitted for standard pret and formal pricing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive 12-column dynamic grid system engineered to celebrate fashion photography through vertical cadence and asymmetrical editorial spreads.

### Breakpoints & Fluid Structure
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content bounding container is clamped at `1440px` for lookbook consistency, allowing hero photography to span `100vw`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `2rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` gutters. Product cards display in a dual-column asymmetric or full-width staggered rhythm.

### Vertical Rhythm & White Space
Sections do not abruptly end; they breathe through large vertical intervals (`space-xl` to double `space-xl` on desktop). Empty space functions as a tangible design element, imbuing value to adjacent garments.

## Elevation & Depth

In keeping with warm editorial minimalism, heavy drop shadows and dramatic physical skeuomorphism are strictly prohibited. Depth is achieved via **tonal stratification**, **sheer scrims**, and **micro-soft ambient diffusions**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background canvas (`#FDFBF7`) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Card):** `#FAF7F2` framed by a 1px border of Silk Sand (`#EFE9E1`). Zero shadow.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** Wishlist buttons, quick-filter bars, and category pills utilize a warm, extra-diffused ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(24, 23, 22, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 23, 22, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Modals):** The floating navigation and cart drawer feature a frosted silk backdrop: `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom border of 1px solid `rgba(223, 194, 184, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

To mirror the pristine, razor-sharp edge of high-fashion publication margins, the architectural shape language adopts a strictly **Sharp (0px)** foundation for containers, images, input fields, and standard buttons.

### Exceptions for Micro-Interactions
- **Pill-Form Elements (`rounded-full`):** Reserved exclusively for micro-tags, floating wishlist circles, COD availability badges, and bespoke sizing chips to communicate tactile interactivity against razor-sharp image grids.
- **Image Ratios:** Strictly standardized to `3:4` (editorial portrait) and `4:5` (e-commerce catalog standard) with razor-sharp 90-degree corners.

## Components

### 1. Announcement Banner
- Ultra-slim ribbon (32px height) atop the canvas.
- Background: Antique Champagne Gold tinted hue (`#FAF7F2`) with a hairline bottom border (`#EFE9E1`).
- Content: Centered `label-caps` text highlighting "Complimentary Nationwide TCS Courier on Orders Over PKR 10,000 | Worldwide DHL Express Available".

### 2. Sticky & Floating Navigation Bar
- Frosted Warm Ivory blur (`#FDFBF7` with 88% opacity and 16px backdrop blur).
- Height: 72px desktop, 60px mobile.
- Center: Regal editorial serif logo mark ("LUXE LANE") set in `Playfair Display`, medium weight, tracking `0.15em`.
- Left: Architectural navigation links (`EASTERN COUTURE`, `WESTERN PRET`, `BRIDAL ATELIER`, `READY TO WEAR`) using `label-caps`.
- Right: Minimalist thin-line icons (Search, Wishlist with live count badge, Shopping Bag).

### 3. Editorial Lookbook Hero
- Full-bleed or generous margin framing (inset 24px) with cinematic photography.
- Asymmetrical text overlay: High-contrast `display-hero` title in Obsidian Noir with an optional italicized serif touchpoint.
- Primary CTA: Obsidian Noir solid block button with crisp uppercase typography and subtle sand-gold hover glow.

### 4. Product Cards
- **Structure:** Aspect ratio `3:4` photograph with an inset background of `#FAF7F2`.
- **Wishlist Action:** Circular floating micro-button in the upper right corner (`#FFFFFF` with soft 4% shadow) housing a fine-line heart icon.
- **Quick-Add Slider:** Slides up seamlessly from the bottom edge on hover, revealing size selectors (`XS`, `S`, `M`, `L`, `XL`, `CUSTOM`) in a stark black bar with white lettering.
- **Pricing & Taxonomy:**
  - Category / Cut label (e.g., "Handcrafted Raw Silk" or "Tailored Linen Blazer") in `label-sm` with charcoal tone.
  - Title in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`).
  - Pricing formatted in `price-md`: `PKR 28,500`.

### 5. Pakistani E-Commerce Contextual Accents
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) Badge:** Soft Sand / Cream pill with a subtle green or obsidian icon dot stating "COD Available".
- **Delivery Notice:** TCS / Courier delivery estimate badge ("Nationwide Delivery: 2–4 Business Days | Stitched to Order: 10–14 Days").
- **Bespoke Sizing Selector:** A dual tab selector letting users toggle between **Standard Sizes** and **Bespoke Eastern Fit** (allowing tailored Kameez Length, Chest, and Trouser measurements).

### 6. Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Button:** `#181716` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, 0px border radius, 48px height, `label-caps` tracking. Hover initiates a transition to Charcoal Slate (`#3D3A37`).
- **Secondary / Atelier Button:** Transparent fill, 1px solid `#181716`, hover transitions to `#181716` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Form Inputs:** Warm Off-White (`#FFFFFF`) background, hairline border in Silk Sand (`#EFE9E1`), focused state elevates to a 1px border of Antique Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`) without aggressive outer rings.
