100% Authentic Silk Guarantee

Govt certified hand-spun threads. Certified genuine Pakistani mulberry raw silk.

Kurta: 100% Pure 80g Raw Silk with hand-applied Resham, Zardozi, and Mukeesh needlework along the band neck, sleeve cuffs, and chaak panels.

Dupatta: 2.75 meters Sheer Pure Organza with antique scalloped Tilla patti and pearl droplets.

Trouser: Pure Raw Silk tailored cigarette pant with pearl detailing at the hem.

Dye Quality: Skin-safe non-carcinogenic pigment vat dying resistant to fading.

Each ensemble requires approximately 48 artisan hours of meticulous needlework in our Old Lahore heritage workshop. Delicate threadwork should be treated with exceptional care.

  • Strictly Dry Clean Only at professional specialized ateliers.
  • Steam iron on reverse using low temperature silk setting.
  • Do not spray perfume directly over metallic tilla or dabka wirework.
  • Store in the provided breathable muslin cotton garment bag.

Features a relaxed, elongated A-line silhouette engineered to flatter authentic South Asian proportions with ease of movement.
